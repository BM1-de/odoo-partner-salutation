## 20.0.1.0.0 (2026-11-19)

- Migration to Odoo 20. What a company is, Odoo 20 computes itself: a
  contact without a parent contact that has a tax ID. Only such a contact
  gets the general greeting; a company without a tax ID is addressed by its
  name, like a person.
